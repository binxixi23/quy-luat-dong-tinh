# Chapter 1: The Law of Supply-Demand and Relative Neutralization

## 1. Yin-Yang and Dynamic Equilibrium
Nature grants us life, and it always possesses two mutually opposing faces: **Matter and Antimatter**, **Dynamics and Stasis**, **Black Holes and White Holes**.
- In a small environment or a brief timeframe (such as the sliver of human history), we might fail to observe this grand balance and mistakenly perceive an absolute asymmetry.
- Looking at the entirety of the system, however, all things inherently drift toward a **relative neutralization** rather than an absolute perfection. Small structural imbalances are merely the necessary drivers keeping the river of energy flowing.

## 2. Demystifying Black Holes Through Supply and Demand
Black holes are heavily romanticized by mainstream media as all-powerful deities. In reality, without a source of "Supply," even a black hole holds no absolute authority:
- **Gravity does not generate power out of nothing:** It merely responds to the exact mass and space (Supply) compressing the system.
- **Action requires reaction (Give and Take):** When the cosmic supply exceeds the maximum processing capacity of the Event Horizon, extreme pressure and magnetic fields force the black hole to "expel toxins"—blasting cosmic winds and plasma jets back into space to restore systemic neutrality.
