# Chapter 4: Critique of 2026 Astronomical Discoveries: Visual Traps and Cosmic Nodes

## 1. Exposing the "Visual Traps" (Gravitational Lensing)
When parsing grand astronomical announcements published in 2026, scientific logic forces us to deconstruct the **Observer's Frame of Reference** looking from Earth:
- **The Abell2744-QSO1 Enigma:** The claim that an ancient primordial black hole contains 2/3 of its galaxy's mass must be viewed with caution. This system is caught directly in a gravitational lens. The massive galaxy cluster in the foreground warps space, splitting and mirroring the single black hole into multiple deceptive visual duplicates.
- **The Triple Black Hole Merger:** The observation of three supermassive black holes swirling together is a classic expression of **"three bubbles caught in a giant whirlpool."** Born out of "The Perfect Time and Place, without Human Intervention" (Thiên thời, Địa lợi, Không có Nhân), the crowded environment of the early universe forced three independent galaxies to collide. Their central black holes are not acting out a planned script; they are simply obeying fluid dynamics on a galactic scale.

## 2. Nested Universes and the Stagnation of Particle Accelerators
- **Universes Within Universes (Matryoshka Cosmology):** The intuition of nested worlds aligns perfectly with *Black Hole Cosmology*. Our entire observable universe—whose radius and mass perfectly match the mathematical criteria of a supermassive black hole—likely burst forth from a "quantum bounce" inside a parent black hole. In turn, the black holes inside our galaxies are currently incubating nested baby universes within their own horizons.
- **The Fading Utility of the Large Hadron Collider (LHC):** The official shutdown of the LHC at CERN on June 29, 2026, marks the natural degeneration of an aging tool. Humans built a 27km ring to simulate a miniature Big Bang, but the laboratory pipe is entirely static and geometrically suffocating. It completely fails to replicate the dynamic, self-generating expansion of real cosmic space. One cannot use a rigid, static formula or a microscopic lab bubble to bind the grand, evolving vortex of the universe.
